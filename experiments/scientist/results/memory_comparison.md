## Verdict on `claim_pastdev_better`

Protocol text: "only if pastdev's wasted tests are lower than ledger's in at least 2 of 3 replicate pairs and its mean later-origin gain is not lower than ledger's. Otherwise 'no advantage shown'."

**No advantage shown.** This verdict does not depend on the missing runs. `ledger r1` and `ledger r2` each wasted 0 tests, and a count cannot be lower than 0, so `pastdev` can be lower than `ledger` in at most 1 of 3 pairs (r3), never 2.
The second clause is moot. For information only: `pastdev r1` (+0.0201) is above both ledger runs (-0.0003, +0.0018), but that is one run against two, and 1 of its 6 kept rules does most of it (`i1`, analog alone on neocloud series, marginal later gain +0.0143; the other five add 0.0000 to +0.0038).
The two `none` replicates differ by 0.0127 in later gain, as much as most between-condition gaps. Nothing here separates the conditions on forecast gain.

What the five runs do suggest (descriptive, 2 to 3 runs per cell, no test): the two conditions that show the model earlier verdicts (ledger, pastdev) proposed 8 candidates for 8 tests with 0 wasted, while `none` proposed 10 (the controller refused 2 as duplicates of its own earlier candidates) and re-tested one already-rejected walk-forward idea in each run. The acceptance line "wasted tests = 0 in some later run" is met (ledger r1, ledger r2, pastdev r1). Memory prevented a repeat before it was proposed rather than after, and a no-memory run still wasted one test per run. Whether `pastdev` retrieval beats simply handing over the whole ledger is not shown: ledger needed about 50% more prompt tokens (21.6k to 21.9k against 14.3k per iteration) for the same wasted-test count.

## Known confound (protocol, `known_confound`)

pastdev recall can return `auto-` records from earlier runs; the ledger condition only has the ledger (written 14 Nov by
earlier research on all practice dates) and this run's own verdicts. An advantage could come from more information, not better
retrieval. Concretely, `pastdev r1` recalled records from the 18 September walk-forward run (`auto-c0918-pastdev-wf-i1, i3, i4,
i5, i6`) and 7 scout records, and, after its own iterations, its own. Further limits: (a) the ledger condition's content
deviates from "nothing dated after C reaches the model" by design (caveat in the protocol); (b) the model has a single draw per
iteration, so replicate variance is what the table shows; (c) `none` still had to write records, which later `pastdev` runs can read.
