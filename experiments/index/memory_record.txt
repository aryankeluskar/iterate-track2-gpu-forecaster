Experiment: index (supported, practice dates only). Claim: a causal provider-balanced matched-offer change index (per collector and GPU model, on-demand rate cards; offers present on both consecutive observed days; one median log change per provider; equal provider weights; min 3 matched providers else carry and flag; chained from the first-day posted median) tracks the same chip more consistently across fastgpu_daily and gpurentalprices than the posted median, without losing responsiveness. Failure mode targeted: median moves from panel membership and single pivotal providers. Frozen test, 11 GPU pairs, target days 2026-09-15..2026-10-06 (242 pair-days): mean abs 7-day collector disagreement index 0.0063 vs posted median 0.0319 (ratio 0.198; paired diff -0.0256, 90% week-block CI -0.048 to -0.011, 4 blocks), balanced median 0.0150; one-provider deletion change 0.0081 vs 0.0309 (lower on all 21 series); Nebius 1 Oct / Jarvislabs 5 Oct: index right direction 14/14 cases, mean retention 0.169 (about 1/N), posted median 11/14; constant index fails; 30/599 invalid days (28 are fastgpu RTX 6000 Ada with 2 providers). Post hoc: correlation of the collectors' 7-day changes 0.72 index vs 0.04 posted. Caveats: small sample; Verda is the most pivotal provider for the index on 17/21 series; agreement is consistency not truth; chained level drifts from the posted level.
